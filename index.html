Privacy Policy

For Calculator Pro · Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Calculator Pro (“the app”) handles information when you use it. Please read it together with any privacy disclosures shown in the app or on its Google Play listing.

1. Information You Provide

The core calculator, GST, and discount tools do not require an account. The app is designed to calculate the values you enter to provide its features. Do not enter confidential or sensitive information into calculation history.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The app may store preferences, such as your selected theme, and recent calculations locally on your device using app or browser storage. This information is used to restore app settings and show your calculation history. You can clear history within the app where that option is available, or clear the app's data through your device settings.

3. Calculations and App Features

Ordinary arithmetic, GST, and discount calculations are intended to be processed on your device. If you use a feature that retrieves online information, such as live currency exchange rates, the relevant request may be sent to the service providing that information. Any such provider may process technical request data under its own privacy policy. The app should disclose any active third-party services in its Google Play Data safety form.

4. Sharing and Sale of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information handled by third-party services, if any are enabled in a version of the app, is subject to the disclosures and privacy terms of those services.

5. Data Security and Retention

Locally stored preferences and history generally remain until you clear them or remove the app or its data. Local storage security depends on your device and app environment. We recommend keeping your device secure and avoiding sensitive information in calculation history.

6. Children's Privacy

Calculator Pro is a general-purpose utility and is not designed to request personal information from children. If the app's features or data practices change, this policy will be updated as needed.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The revised version will be published at this URL with an updated date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the developer contact details provided on the Calculator Pro Google Play Store listing.

Developer reminder: Before publishing the app, make sure this policy matches the final app and every SDK or service it uses, including any advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or live currency-rate provider. Complete the Google Play Data safety form accurately.
