Privacy Policy

For Calculator Pro · Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Calculator Pro (“the app”) handles information when you use it. The policy below assumes the app performs calculations on your device and does not include advertising, analytics, accounts, or third-party data collection. Please update this policy if your published app uses any such services.

1. Information We Collect

Calculator Pro does not ask you to create an account and, in its current configuration, does not collect or send personal information to us. Calculations you enter are processed on your device.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The app may use your device’s local storage to remember preferences and features such as recent calculation history and your selected theme. This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to us by the app. You can clear calculation history using the controls provided in the app, or clear the app’s local data through your device settings.

3. How We Use Information

Locally stored information is used only to provide app features, such as restoring your preferred theme and showing recent calculations. We do not sell personal information.

4. Sharing and Third-Party Services

In its current configuration, Calculator Pro does not share personal information with third parties. If a future version adds third-party services, such as analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or online conversion APIs, this policy should be updated to explain what information those services receive and why.

5. Data Security and Retention

Calculation history and preferences stored locally are subject to the security of your device and browser/app environment. They generally remain until you clear them or remove the app or its data. Do not store sensitive information in calculation history.

6. Children’s Privacy

Calculator Pro is a general-purpose calculator and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Since the app does not collect personal information in its current configuration, it does not knowingly collect such information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app’s features or data practices change. Any updated version will be published at the same privacy-policy URL with a revised “Last updated” date.
